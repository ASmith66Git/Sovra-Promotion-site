      <h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
      <p class="updated">Last updated: April 22, 2026</p>

      <p>
        This Privacy Policy explains how <strong>Leviathan Systems Ltd</strong>
        ("we", "us", "our"), a company registered in England and Wales,
        collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Sovra
        mobile application and related services (the "Service").
      </p>

      <h2>1. Summary &amp; Privacy by Design</h2>
      <p>The Service is built on the principle of data sovereignty.</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>On-Device Storage:</strong> Your messages, attachments, and notes are stored in encrypted storage on your physical device.</li>
        <li><strong>Transient Processing:</strong> Our servers act as a relay. We do not store the content of your communications on our backend.</li>
        <li><strong>Zero-Knowledge:</strong> We have no technical means to access your decrypted user data.</li>
        <li><strong>No Data Sales:</strong> We do not sell your data or use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>2. Information We Access</h2>
      <p>Depending on which integrations you enable, the Service may access the following:</p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Gmail:</strong> Email messages, threads, attachments, labels, and your basic Google profile (name, email, profile picture).</li>
        <li><strong>iCloud Mail (IMAP):</strong> Message data from folders you connect.</li>
        <li><strong>Contacts:</strong> Used strictly for displaying sender identities within the app.</li>
        <li><strong>Subscription Data:</strong> Processed via <strong>RevenueCat</strong>. On iOS, we use the Identifier for Vendor (IDFV) solely for fraud prevention and subscription management. It is not used for advertising.</li>
        <li><strong>Diagnostic Logs:</strong> Minimal technical traces (error logs) to maintain Service integrity.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>3. Google User Data &amp; Limited Use Disclosure</h2>
      <div class="callout">
        <strong>Sovra's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the
        <a href="https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google API Services User Data Policy</a>,
        including the Limited Use requirements.</strong>
      </div>

      <h3>Scopes we request</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><code>gmail.modify</code> &mdash; to fetch and display your messages, threads, labels, and attachments, and to mark messages as read, archive, or label them based on actions you take in the app.</li>
        <li><code>gmail.send</code> &mdash; to send replies, forwards, and new messages that you compose.</li>
        <li><code>userinfo.profile</code> and <code>userinfo.email</code> &mdash; to identify your Google account inside the app.</li>
      </ul>

      <h3>How Google user data is handled</h3>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>No AI Training:</strong> Google user data is <strong>not</strong> used to train, develop, or improve any generalized AI or Machine Learning models.</li>
        <li><strong>No Human Access:</strong> No employee or contractor reads your Gmail data except (a) with your explicit consent for support, (b) to investigate abuse, or (c) where required by law.</li>
        <li><strong>Relay Storage:</strong> Email content held in server memory during relay operations is immediately discarded and never written to disk.</li>
        <li><strong>Storage:</strong> Gmail message bodies, headers, and attachments are stored in encrypted on-device storage so the app can work offline. They are not stored in our backend database.</li>
        <li><strong>Prohibited Uses:</strong> Gmail data is never sold or shared with third parties for advertising, credit determination, or any purpose unrelated to providing the user-facing features of Sovra. It will not be transferred to or used by data brokers, consumer reporting agencies, or any party for such purposes.</li>
      </ul>

      <h3>Revoking Google access</h3>
      <p>
        You can disconnect your Google account from inside the app at any time, or
        revoke access directly at
        <a href="https://myaccount.google.com/permissions" target="_blank" rel="noopener">myaccount.google.com/permissions</a>.
        Revoking access stops further sync and removes your tokens from our backend.
      </p>

      <h2>4. Data Storage and Retention</h2>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Local Data:</strong> All decrypted message content stays on your device.</li>
        <li><strong>Backend Metadata:</strong> We store only OAuth refresh tokens and minimal account-link metadata (email address).</li>
        <li><strong>Retention:</strong> OAuth tokens are permanently deleted upon account disconnection or deletion. We do not retain tokens beyond the life of your active integration.</li>
        <li><strong>Third-party processors:</strong> Replit (US) hosts our backend infrastructure. RevenueCat processes subscription receipts.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>5. Your Rights and Account Deletion</h2>
      <p>
        Under the <strong>UK GDPR</strong>, you have rights including access,
        rectification, and erasure.
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Account Deletion:</strong> You can delete your account and all associated backend data at any time via the <strong>"Delete Account"</strong> button in the App Settings or by emailing <a href="mailto:privacy@leviathan-sys.com">privacy@leviathan-sys.com</a>.</li>
        <li><strong>Revocation:</strong> You may revoke API access at any time via your Google or Apple account security settings.</li>
        <li><strong>Access and export:</strong> Contact us at the address below to request a copy of any personal data we hold on our backend.</li>
        <li>Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have additional rights under the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, or CCPA &mdash; we will honour verified requests as required by law.</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>6. Security</h2>
      <p>
        We utilise industry-standard TLS for data in transit and OS-level secure
        storage (Keychain on iOS) for credentials on-device. All user data stored
        locally is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. No system is perfectly secure;
        please use a device passcode and keep your operating system up to date.
      </p>
